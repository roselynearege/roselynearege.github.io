About Me

I am a passionate ICT teacher dedicated to helping learners develop digital skills, creativity, and problem-solving abilities. I create engaging and practical learning experiences that enable students to apply technology confidently in real-life situations.

My teaching focuses on developing learners' understanding of Information and Communication Technology, computer applications, programming, and digital literacy. I strive to create an inclusive learning environment where students are encouraged to explore, collaborate, and achieve their full potential.

I am committed to continuous professional development and the effective integration of technology into teaching and learning. My goal is to empower learners with the knowledge and skills they need to thrive in an increasingly digital world.
